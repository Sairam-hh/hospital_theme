Our Story

25 Years of Healing, Hope & Innovation

CareFirst Hospital was founded in 2001 with a single mission: to make world-class healthcare accessible to every patient. Starting with 50 beds in Chennai, we have grown into a 800-bed multi-specialty hospital trusted by over 95,000 patients annually.

Our state-of-the-art infrastructure includes robotic surgery theatres, advanced diagnostic imaging, a dedicated cancer centre, and an internationally accredited ICU.
